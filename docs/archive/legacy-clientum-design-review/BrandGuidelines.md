# Brand Guidelines

ClientumOS is built on a foundation of Clarity, Trust, and Progress. Our design system is a direct reflection of these pillars, engineered to empower PyMEs with an efficient, professional, and accessible CRM ecosystem.

## Core Visual Pillars
- **Clarity**: High-contrast, logical layouts with ample negative space.
- **Trust**: Solid, institutional color palettes (Navy `#022046` / Corporate Blue `#002B5C`) ensuring professional seriousness.
- **Progress**: Intuitive, action-oriented interface with clear paths (Action Blue `#0056B3`) to success.

## Typographical Standards
- **Primary Font**: Inter (Usage: H1-H3, Body, CTA)
- **Alternative Font**: Arial (Fallback)

## Color Palette
- **Navy**: `#022046`
- **Corporate Blue**: `#002B5C`
- **Action Blue**: `#0056B3`
- **Success Green**: `#4CAF50`
- **Surface**: `#F5F7FA`
